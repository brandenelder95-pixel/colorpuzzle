Privacy Policy for Color Sort Puzzle

Last updated: September 14, 2026

FreeLifeGames ("we," "us," or "our") operates Color Sort Puzzle (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

The App stores gameplay information and preferences on your device. This may include level progress, coins, hints, power-ups, missions, streaks, achievements, sound settings, and other game settings. The App also stores a small local history of gameplay, ad, and purchase events for app operation and diagnostics.

You can remove this locally stored information by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your device settings.

Anonymous gameplay synchronization

To help preserve gameplay-earned coin progress, the App creates an anonymous random player identifier and stores it securely on your device when possible. The App sends that identifier and the gameplay-earned coin total to our server. We do not use this identifier to identify you by name, email address, or other direct contact information.

We use this information only to synchronize gameplay-earned coins and protect the integrity of the in-game coin ledger.

Advertising and consent

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, app-open, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising or performance information under their own policies.

Where required, the App asks for consent through Google's privacy consent tools. Your choice controls whether ads are personalized or non-personalized; ads may still be shown when personalized advertising is not authorized. Purchasing Remove Ads hides interruptive ads, while optional rewarded ads may remain available for in-game rewards.

Google's privacy information is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The App offers optional digital in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. RevenueCat helps the App process purchase entitlements and restore purchases. We receive purchase and entitlement information needed to grant digital items and Remove Ads, but Google Play processes your payment details. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

RevenueCat's privacy information is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

How we use information

We use the information described above to provide gameplay, restore purchases, synchronize gameplay-earned coins, show advertisements, honor privacy choices, maintain app security, and troubleshoot reliability issues.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Service providers

Google Play, Google Mobile Ads, and RevenueCat may process information under their own privacy policies to provide billing, advertising, consent, fraud prevention, and related services. These providers may process information on servers outside your country.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Data retention and deletion requests

Device-stored game data remains until you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Anonymous gameplay coin records are retained only as needed to provide synchronization and protect the game economy. You may request deletion of an anonymous server record by contacting us. We may need enough information to identify the relevant record, but we will not ask for payment credentials.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact FreeLifeGames@outlook.com.
